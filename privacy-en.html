Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026 · futbolik (package net.futbolik.app)

In short

futbolik has no accounts. We don't ask you to register, and we don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. The app collects nothing that identifies you and sends nothing about you to us.

The app shows ads. Google (AdMob) serves them, and Google collects some device information to make them work. Exactly what that means is below.

Ads and Google AdMob

There is a banner ad at the bottom of the app, and optional rewarded ads you can watch to unlock certain sections. These ads are served by Google AdMob.

To serve, measure, and protect ads from abuse, Google may process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical details
  • which ad was served, viewed, or clicked

This data is processed on Google's servers under Google's own privacy policy. For details see how Google uses data in advertising.

You control your advertising ID. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset it or delete it entirely. Deleting it does not remove ads, but they will no longer be personalised to you.

What's stored on your device

A few things are stored only on your device so the app can work. None of it is sent to us:

  • Match data — the statistics file downloaded once a day, kept so the app works without a connection.
  • Theme preference — your light/dark choice.
  • Unlock timer — if you watched a rewarded ad, when the section you unlocked expires.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it from your device.

The app's language follows your device's system language. That choice is never stored; it is read from the device each time the app opens.

Where the data comes from

The fixtures and statistical calculations you see come from a single data file downloaded from a public address. That file is refreshed once a day and is identical for everyone — it does not vary by who downloads it, it is not personalised, and nothing about you is sent when it is downloaded.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, request its correction or deletion, and object to its processing. Because we hold no record that identifies you, there is no data we can match to your request; we review every request regardless and respond within 30 days at the latest.

For requests about the advertising data Google processes, you need to contact Google directly — we have no access to it.

futbolik is not directed at people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
