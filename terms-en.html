How to read these numbers

The percentages in the app are statistical calculations derived from past match data. They are the output of a calculation, not a tipster's opinion.

An outcome looking likely does not mean it will happen. Something shown at 89% will, over the long run, fail to happen in roughly one out of every nine cases — that is not an error, it is what a probability means.
